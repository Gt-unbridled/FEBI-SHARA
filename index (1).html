Meet Febi

The woman behind the vision.

FEBi came from my love for fashion and African culture. I wanted to create pieces that don’t just look African, but actually tell a story through the fabrics and details.

I’m reinterpreting our heritage and culture in a way that feels modern and exciting for today’s woman.
